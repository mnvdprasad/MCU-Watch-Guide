# 🎬 MCU Watch Guide

</div>

🌐 **Live Website:** https://mcuwatchguide.vercel.app

### The Ultimate Watch Guide & True Marvel Experience

**MCU Watch Guide** is an independent, unofficial Marvel viewing reference created to bring Marvel's extensive screen universe into one structured, intuitive, and easy-to-navigate experience.

Explore titles by **Release Order**, **Chronological Order**, or **Browse All**, while keeping track of your personal viewing progress directly in your browser.

---

## 🌌 About the Project

Marvel's screen universe spans decades of films, television series, specials, one-shots, and animated productions across multiple franchises, timelines, and continuities.

**MCU Watch Guide** organizes this extensive collection into a unified viewing reference, making it easier to discover titles, understand their placement, and decide what to watch next.

The guide distinguishes between the **Marvel Cinematic Universe** and selected **Legacy / Non-MCU productions**, providing a broader Marvel reference while keeping different continuities and franchises clearly organized.

Whether you are beginning your Marvel journey or revisiting the larger universe, MCU Watch Guide is designed to make the experience clear, organized, and easy to follow.

---

## ✨ Features

### 📅 Multiple Viewing Orders

Choose the viewing experience that suits you:

- 🗂️ **Browse All** — Explore the collection through its structured categories.
- 📅 **Release Order** — Follow titles according to their release sequence.
- ⏳ **Chronological Order** — Explore titles according to their position within the story timeline.

The selected sorting preference is stored locally in the browser.

---

### 🔎 Instant Search

Find titles quickly using the built-in search.

Search results update dynamically as you type, allowing you to locate specific films, series, specials, or other productions without manually browsing the complete collection.

---

### 🎞️ Content Filters

Quickly narrow the collection using dedicated filters:

- 🎬 **Movies**
- 📺 **TV Series**
- ✨ **Specials**
- 👁️ **Unwatched**

The filtering system works together with the search and sorting controls to provide a focused browsing experience.

---

### ✅ Personal Watch Tracking

Mark individual titles as **Watched** as you progress through the guide.

Your viewing progress is stored locally in your browser, so no account is required to maintain your watch history.

The guide automatically restores your saved watched status when you return to the website.

---

### 📊 Watch Progress

The interface provides visual progress indicators for different content categories:

- 🎬 Films
- 📺 TV & Animated Series
- ✨ Specials & One-Shots

Each category displays watched titles relative to the total available titles through a compact circular progress indicator.

## 🎨 Design Philosophy

MCU Watch Guide is designed around a simple principle:

> **The interface should help the content — not compete with it.**

The visual system combines a dark cinematic interface with restrained accent colors and structured content presentation.

Key design characteristics include:

- 🌑 Dark cinematic interface
- 🟥 Marvel-inspired red accents
- 🟡 Category-specific visual indicators
- 🖼️ Poster-based content presentation
- 🧊 Glass-style interface elements
- ⭕ Circular progress indicators
- ✨ Subtle transitions and animations
- 📱 Responsive layouts
- 💨 Lightweight interaction patterns

The objective is to create a focused viewing companion rather than an unnecessarily complicated interface.

## 🔄 Dynamic Filtering & Sorting

MCU Watch Guide provides dynamic filtering and sorting controls to help users navigate the collection efficiently.

The interface combines:

- 🔎 **Search** — Find titles by name.
- 🏷️ **Content Filtering** — Filter titles by content type.
- 📅 **Release Order** — Display titles according to their assigned release-order ranking.
- ⏳ **Chronological Order** — Display titles according to their associated timeline year.
- 🗂️ **Browse All** — Explore the collection through its structured categories.
- 👁️ **Unwatched** — Display titles that have not yet been marked as watched.

Search, filtering, and sorting work together dynamically, allowing the displayed collection to update without requiring a page reload.

The selected sorting preference is also stored locally so the preferred viewing mode can be retained between visits.

## 🔐 Privacy & Local Storage

MCU Watch Guide does not require an account for personal watch tracking.

Watched titles and selected viewing preferences are stored locally in the user's browser using the browser's `localStorage` functionality.

### Local Data

The website may store:

- ✅ Watched titles
- 📅 Selected sorting preference

### Important Considerations

- 🔒 No account is required for watch tracking.
- 💻 Watch progress is stored in the current browser.
- 🌐 Progress does not automatically synchronize between devices or browsers.
- 🧹 Clearing the website's stored browser data can remove saved progress.
- 🕵️ Private or incognito browsing may not preserve local data after the session.

Watch history is stored locally under the `marvel_watched` key.

For complete information regarding data handling and privacy, please refer to the website's **Privacy Policy**.
